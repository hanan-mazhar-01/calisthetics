How these would go into the app
  • The four drawings (24 × 24, round 1.8 px line) become small vector assets. Colour comes from the theme at draw time: idle textSecondary, active onAccent on the rose pill, second tone onAccent at about 34 % (the soft fill). One set of shapes serves both themes.
  • The bar itself does not change: same floating glass pill, drag-to-switch, active tab widens into the rose pill with its label.
  • Tab names stay Plan, Discover, Progress, Profile.
